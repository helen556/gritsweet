Data is processed to respond to enquiries, agree and fulfil orders, arrange delivery, make payments, keep records, handle claims, and ensure the operation and security of the website.

To the extent necessary, data may be provided to the carrier, the bank or payment provider, hosting, e-mail and technical maintenance providers, and persons engaged in lawful accounting or legal support.

5. Retention periods

Data is kept for as long as needed to fulfil the relevant purpose, the contract and legal requirements, including those relating to accounting documents and the protection of rights in disputes.

You have the right to obtain information about the processing of your data, to access it, to request correction of inaccuracies, to object to processing and to request erasure in the cases provided for by law.

Send requests to vinor.trade@gmail.com. To protect your data we may ask for information sufficient to confirm the identity of the requester.

You may lodge a complaint with the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights or apply to a court.

7. Data security

We take organisational and technical measures to protect data from unauthorised access, loss or disclosure. Access is granted only to persons who need it to perform the relevant tasks.

8. Cookies and local storage

The website does not set its own cookies and does not use analytics or advertising services (including Google Analytics or Meta Pixel). A separate cookie consent banner is therefore not needed.

To operate the cart, the website stores its contents in your browser’s sessionStorage (key vinor-cart-uk or vinor-cart-en). This data is not transmitted to the Seller, contains no personal data and is deleted automatically when the browser tab is closed.

The website is hosted on Vercel, whose infrastructure processes technical request data (IP address, request time, browser information) to operate and protect the website. Page fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so when you open the website your browser contacts Google’s servers and sends them technical request data.
